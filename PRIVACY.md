# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** 1 August 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to **GIF Sender for WhatsApp**.

## Overview

GIF Sender for WhatsApp is designed to process media files entirely on your device. The extension does not collect, store, or transmit your personal data to the developer or any third party.

## Information We Process

When you use the extension, you may choose to drag and drop or select media files, including GIF, WebP, APNG, MP4, WebM and MOV files.

These files are processed locally within your browser solely to convert them into a format compatible with WhatsApp Web.

The extension does not upload, store, or retain copies of your files.

## Data Collection

GIF Sender for WhatsApp does **not** collect:

* Personal information
* Account information
* Authentication credentials
* Messages or chat history
* Browsing history
* Location information
* Analytics or usage statistics
* Telemetry

## Data Sharing

The extension does not sell, share, transfer, or disclose user data to the developer or any third party.

When you choose to send converted media, it is uploaded directly to WhatsApp Web as part of WhatsApp's normal message sending process. This communication occurs between your browser and WhatsApp and is not routed through or accessible by the extension's developer.

## Permissions

The extension only runs on **https://web.whatsapp.com/**.

Access to WhatsApp Web is required only to:

* Detect user-initiated drag-and-drop and file selection.
* Display the extension's user interface.
* Convert supported media locally.
* Insert the converted media into WhatsApp Web's standard upload flow.

The extension does not access or operate on other websites.

## Third-Party Software

GIF Sender for WhatsApp may include third-party open-source libraries. These libraries are packaged with the extension and execute locally within your browser.

## Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the extension's functionality changes. Any updates will be published in this repository.
